manifesting this intention will be at the core of everything that we do,
that does include mission, vision and values. And it starts by influencing those
by keeping it real, instead of sounding pretentious like it does
in the examples you send me.

Mission:

Creating the best TCG
by turning players into creators
and allowing everyone around the world
to participate in the games evolution
transforming the game into a global
effort and phenomenon.

Vision:

Reforming the industry by creating a TCG where players
not just play but co-create the game, too.

- developing a software that allows player, upon the fundation we provide,
  to build the best TCG ever created.

Values:

- Honesty: With us the players and creators get what the see.
	   We openly communicate our price structures
	   and revenue streams.

- Trust: We give the players and creators the feeling
	 that their voicec and feeling are heard, cherished
	 and that they actively inform our design decision.

- Community: All of us create the game together.
	     It is ours from the start and we all
	     share it.





- no predatory advertising, marketing
- no revenue structures like slot machines
- no internet connection, unless it is absolutely necessary
- no pack filler cards/every card has a purepose
- no/very little power creep
- user voices are heared, respected and valued
- we create a game by the community for the community
